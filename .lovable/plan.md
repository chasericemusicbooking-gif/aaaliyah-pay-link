# Refine checkout and card verification

## Changes
- Remove decorative gradients and glass-like styling for a restrained luxury payment interface.
- Expand card payment into card-type selection, card details, and a timed six-digit verification step.
- Let staff configure the card verification window, defaulting to 10 minutes.
- Keep the experience explicitly in demo mode; card details and codes are not stored.
- Validate the updated mobile flow and current build.
